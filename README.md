A 360° marketing campaign performance analysis project using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI** to evaluate advertising spend, reach, engagement, conversions, revenue, and campaign efficiency across products, categories, marketing channels, and time.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **1,000 marketing campaign records** covering the period from **12 November 2024 to 9 February 2025**.

The analysis evaluates campaign performance through:

- Advertising spend
- Revenue
- Impressions
- Clicks
- Conversions
- CTR
- Conversion Rate
- CPM
- ROAS
- Product performance
- Category performance
- Marketing channel performance
- Monthly trends

The project transforms campaign-level data into an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that allows stakeholders to explore marketing performance and compare different categories.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

Marketing teams need a consolidated view of where advertising budget is being spent and how efficiently that spending generates revenue.

The objective of this project is to provide a single analytical view that helps stakeholders:

- Monitor overall marketing campaign performance
- Evaluate advertising efficiency
- Track the marketing funnel
- Compare monthly performance
- Analyze category performance
- Compare marketing channels
- Identify high- and low-performing products
- Monitor ROAS, CTR, conversion rate and CPM
- Explore performance through an interactive Power BI dashboard

---

# ❓ Business Questions

The analysis addresses the following questions:

1. How much advertising spend was used across the campaign period?
2. How much revenue was attributed to the campaigns?
3. What was the overall ROAS?
4. How efficiently did impressions convert into clicks?
5. How efficiently did clicks convert into sales?
6. Which marketing channels generated stronger ROAS?
7. Which categories generated stronger revenue and conversion performance?
8. Which products generated the highest revenue?
9. Which products had lower ROAS?
10. How did campaign performance change month by month?
11. How does marketing spend compare with revenue and conversions?
12. Which areas require further investigation?

# 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Campaign Efficiency

The campaign program generated **₹142.51 in revenue for every ₹1 of advertising spend**.

However, performance varies considerably across marketing channels and products. Channel-level ROAS ranges from **131.89 to 157.07**, while product-level ROAS ranges from **85.65 to 259.64**.

This demonstrates why overall ROAS should be supplemented with channel, category and product-level analysis.

### 2. Instagram Ads Performance

Instagram Ads recorded:

- ROAS: **157.07**
- CPM: **₹8.79**
- Spend Share: **18.96%**
- Revenue Share: **20.89%**

The channel generated a higher share of revenue than its share of advertising spend.

### 3. Household Category Performance

Household recorded:

- Revenue: **₹82.83M**
- Revenue Share: **24.05%**
- ROAS: **157.18**
- Conversion Rate: **11.25%**
- Ad Spend: **₹526,944**

Household represented the largest revenue share among the five categories.

### 4. Personal Care Performance

Personal Care recorded:

- Revenue: **₹61.97M**
- Revenue Share: **18.00%**
- ROAS: **125.68**
- Conversion Rate: **8.88%**
- Ad Spend: **₹493,063**

The category should be investigated further to understand the differences in creative, targeting and channel mix.

### 5. High-ROAS Products

The highest product-level ROAS values included:

| Product | ROAS |
|---|---:|
| Air Freshener | 259.64 |
| Eggs | 242.02 |
| Biscuits | 195.07 |

These products also appear among the higher-revenue products in the portfolio.

### 6. Lower-ROAS Products

The lowest product-level ROAS values included:

| Product | ROAS |
|---|---:|
| Toothpaste | 85.65 |
| Chips | 88.03 |
| Energy Drink | 95.29 |

Chips also recorded the lowest total revenue among the 25 products.

### 7. Monthly Performance

December 2024 and January 2025 together accounted for:

- **68.6% of total advertising spend**
- **71.6% of total revenue**

January 2025 recorded the highest ROAS among the four available monthly periods at **151.29**.

### 8. Conversion Rate vs ROAS

Referral and Influencer Marketing recorded the highest channel-level conversion rates:

- Influencer Marketing: **10.19%**
- Referral: **9.96%**

However, conversion rate and ROAS do not always move together.

This demonstrates the importance of evaluating multiple marketing metrics together.

### 9. CTR Stability

CTR remained relatively stable across the campaign period and channels, ranging from approximately **4.67% to 5.22%**.

The larger performance differences appear further down the funnel through conversion efficiency and ROAS.

### 10. February Data Limitation

February 2025 contains only **9 days of campaign data**.

Therefore, the lower February totals and ROAS should not be treated as evidence of a full-month decline without additional data.
